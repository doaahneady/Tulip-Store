===============================================================================
IMPORTANT NOTES
===============================================================================

1. Mart products use SESSION storage, not database
2. Session key: 'mart_products'
3. Weight-based products get unique IDs: productId_timestamp_random
4. Each weight purchase is a separate cart item
5. Exchange rate: 1 USD = 13,100 SYP (configurable)
6. Products stored in USD, displayed in SYP
7. Weight units detected: kilogram, gram, كيلو, كيلوغرام, غرام, كيلو غرام
